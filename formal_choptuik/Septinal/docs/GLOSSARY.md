## Critical collapse

**Choptuik critical collapse (критический коллапс Чоптуика)** — the
gravitational collapse of a massless scalar field at the threshold of black-hole
formation: below a critical initial amplitude `p*` the field disperses, above it
a black hole forms, and the mass obeys `M_BH ∝ |p − p*|^γ` with a universal
exponent γ ≈ 0.374 (Choptuik 1993).

**DSS — discretely self-similar (дискретная самоподобность)** — the symmetry of
the critical solution: under `(τ, ρ) ↦ (τ + Δ, e^{−Δ} ρ)` the fields repeat
exactly, `ψ(τ+Δ, ρ) = e^{−κΔ} ψ(τ, ρ e^{Δ})`. The log-time step `Δ` is the
**echoing period**; each repetition is an **echo (эхо)**.

**CSS — continuously self-similar** — the continuous limit of DSS (Δ → 0);
in the package, the fixed point of the DSS boundary analysis: CSS ⇔ constant
fields (`Existence.DSSBoundary.css_iff_constant`).

**Wiggle (виггля)** — the periodic oscillation of the black-hole mass around
the pure power law, generated by the DSS echoing; its period in `ln(1/δ)` is
`λ₀Δ/2 = 4.6074…` for the calibrated constants.

**Double-null coordinates (координаты двойных нулей)** — the arena
`ds² = −e^{2f(u,v)} du dv + r(u,v)² dΩ²` with ingoing/outgoing null coordinates
`u, v`; the natural coordinates for collapse (`Geometry.Basic`).

**Hawking mass (масса Хокинга)** — the quasi-local mass of the spheres of the
double-null foliation; the package proves both derivative identities and both
monotonicity laws (`Analysis.FieldEquations.hawking_mass_*`).

**Strong / isotropic singularity** — a singularity where the Kretschmann
scalar blows up along every approach (strong) while the Weyl amplitude stays
globally bounded relative to curvature (isotropic); proved from the two
curvature certificates (`Geometry.Curvature`).

## Stability and the bridge

**Unstable mode λ₀ (неустойчивая мода)** — the single eigenvalue of the
linearized Einstein–scalar operator with positive real part; `λ₀ = 2.6745`,
and `γ = 1/λ₀` — the kernel of the scaling law.

**Echo family (эховое семейство)** — the infinite tower of modes generated by
the echoing; its imaginary parts sit at odd multiples of the fundamental
frequency, spacing `2π/Δ` (`Stability.echo_family_structure`).

**Gershgorin disc theorem** — the classical eigenvalue enclosure used to
bracket spectra of the discretized operator (`Stability.Gershgorin`).

**Newton–Kantorovich theorem** — the quantitative local theorem: if the
inverse derivative bound is β, the Lipschitz constant of the residual is L,
the residual at the center is r, and `βLr/(1−βL) ≤ R`, a unique zero exists in
the ball of radius R. The package's engine for existence results
(`Existence.ImplicitFunction`, `Existence.TruncatedDSS`).

**Certificate bridge (сертификатный мост)** — the discipline of moving
numerical claims into Lean: Python emits **exact rational enclosures**
(lo/hi fractions), `Generated.lean` re-checks them by `norm_num`, and
`Discharge.lean` turns them into theorems. Four kinds: `spectrum`, `newton`,
`scaling`, `interval`.

**Interval soundness (корректность интервалов)** — the proved laws that an
interval operation's enclosure contains the real result for every real input
(`Analysis.Interval.add_sound`, `mul_sound`).

## The Septinial satellite

**Septinial braking satellite (септинальный тормозной спутник)** — sector C of
the parent programme: the reading of the spinor-correction constants on the
geometry of the Klein quartic curve.

**The dial (диск)** — the 24-slot cycle (P1: one turn = 24 slots, identified
with the 24 heptagons of the Klein quartic) with one unremovable antipodal gap
{0, 12} of width 2 (P2); the effective capacity is `k = 24 − 2 = 22`.

**k = 22 = b₂(K3) (структурный делитель)** — the effective dial capacity,
identified with the second Betti number of a K3 surface (`20 + 2h^{20} = 22`);
fixed before any fitting, the structural divisor of the braking quantum
α(d) = d⁵/22.

**Chirality (хиральность)** — postulate P3: the dial is an *oriented* cycle;
the mirror `x ↦ 24 − x` swaps the two 11-slot arcs — chirality is the choice of
orientation, not of counting.

**Cascade 48 → 4 → 2 (каскад)** — the symmetry breaking of the dial (P4): of
the 48 dihedral maps of the 24-cycle, exactly the four `{x, x+12, −x, 12−x}`
preserve the gap (the Klein four-group V₄); the orientation keeps the two
rotations — the Z₂ tail.

**The three corrections (три поправки)** — ONE structural pair
β(d) = d²/2 (Berry, 1st order) and α(d) = d⁵/22 (braking, 2nd order) applied
to the three septinial phases δ_A = π/2, δ_B = π/3, δ_C = π/7; the integer
skeleton: `2⁵·22 = 704`, `3⁵·22 = 5346`, `7⁵·22 = 369754`.

**b_Ch (чоптуиковская константа сектора C)** — `1 − cos(2π/7) = 0.376510…`,
bracketed in Lean by `1 − √2/2 < b_Ch < 1/2`.

**γ₃ — the three-term record (трёхчленная запись)** —
`γ₃ = b_Ch − π/1200 = 0.373892…`, an exact, parameter-free model prediction for
the critical exponent; π/1200 is the vibration carrier (1200 = 2⁴·75).

**Berry quantum (квант Берри)** — the leading shift of the two-level normal
form: `λ₋(δ) = (1 − √(1+2δ²))/2 = −δ²/2 + R(δ)` with the proved remainder
`0 < R(δ) ≤ δ⁴/4`.

**PSL(2,7) = GL(3,2)** — the simple group of order 168 = the automorphism
group of the Klein quartic; element orders `1 + 21 + 56 + 42 + 48`.

**Klein quartic {7,3} map (карта {7,3})** — the regular tiling of the
genus-3 Klein quartic by 24 heptagons (56 vertices, 84 edges, χ = −4); the
canonical antipodal structure splits the 24 slots into 8 triangles joined by
24 privileged pairs (setwise stabilizer 7 — the maximum over all 276 pairs).

**K3 bridge (мост K3)** — the arithmetic identity `1 + 22 + 1 = 24` connecting
the dial (24 slots, k = 22) with the K3 surface (b₂ = 22 between the two
generators of H²).

## Mechanics

**`sorry` / `sorryAx`** — Lean's proof placeholder; the package contains none
in proof terms (audit-verified).

**propext · Classical.choice · Quot.sound** — the three standard axioms of
Lean's kernel; the only axioms in any dependency tree of the package.

**`norm_num`** — the mathlib tactic performing exact rational arithmetic; the
workhorse of the certificate checks and of every closed-form constant
(`2⁵·22 = 704` and friends).

**seed 1993** — the package determinism stamp, inherited from the parent
programme: every regenerated artifact is byte-identical.
