Three research monographs that develop the formalization programme in
narrative depth — figures, formulas, tables and references included.
Each volume ships in **four** renditions: Russian and English, DOCX and
PDF, identical content per language.

## Volume I — *From Code to Machine-Checked Proof: a Formalization Roadmap for Choptuik Critical Phenomena*

The full-arc volume: the Choptuik problem and why it deserves a proof;
the mathematical foundations (DSS, scaling, singularity structure); the
classified gap analysis; the three-phase roadmap; the Lean 4 + mathlib
case; and the Lean⇄Python bridge architecture.

## Volume II — *Gap Analysis and Proof-Assistant Selection for the Choptuik Formalization Programme*

The audit volume: the present state of the parent programme; the
detailed registry G1–G6; the central blocker (existence) dissected;
stability and spectral requirements; the full Isabelle/HOL vs Coq/Rocq
vs Lean 4 comparison; and the technical specification with the
axiomatic base.

## Volume III — *The Certificate Channel and the Architecture of Trust*

The certificate-channel volume: the audited state of the programme;
verified interval arithmetic (G5); the typed bridge and its seven-method
protocol (G4); the certificate channel end to end — four kinds, the
generated Lean module, the discharge theorems; the existence route (G1);
the stability route (G2); the geometry of the singularity (G3); the
quality infrastructure; and appendices — glossary, module map, the
headline-theorem ledger and a one-session verification checklist.

## Reading order

1. Volume I, ch. 1–2 — the physics and the stakes.
2. Volume II, ch. 3 — the gap ledger, in depth.
3. Volume I, ch. 4 — the roadmap that answers the ledger.
4. Volume II, ch. 6–7 — why Lean, and under which axioms.
5. Volume III — the architecture of trust: how numerics enter proofs
   legally, and what is already discharged.
