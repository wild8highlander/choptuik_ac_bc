## What this directory is

`monograph/` is the scholarly layer of the package: two reference
volumes, each shipped in Russian and English, each as an editable DOCX
and a print-ready PDF:

| volume | RU | EN | content |
|--------|----|----|---------|
| **Volume I: The Theorems** | `theorems_ru` | `theorems_en` | 18 sections: the riddle and the four-link proposal; **thirteen theorems T1–T13** with statements, proofs, machine verification and statuses (T11 the stability contrast, T12 the invariant wall and T13 the static barrier are the v1.2.0 additions); the verification matrix (11 campaigns + the IVP-11 audit); the honesty register (the 27-claim Evidence Engine registry); references |
| **Volume II: The Appendix** | `appendix_ru` | `appendix_en` | 14 parts: the constants registry, the G-model, the trio engine (with the T13 bound), the W-model, campaigns C1–C8, **campaigns C9–C11 and the Evidence Engine** (the v1.2.0 part), the T1–T13 theorems summary, the bridge and both contact points, the data dictionary (32 tables), the figure catalogue (40 + 100 hires), the multilang matrix (14/14 ×3), the reproduction runbook, the roadmap, the FAQ |

The two volumes cross-reference each other: the appendix's part VII is
the summary register of the theorems, and every theorem names its
campaign verdicts and test pins.  Print PDFs live in `pdf/`.

## The cover

Both volumes use the CHB "Ink & Gold" identity (IG-1 palette, R2
double-rule cover): dark ink background, two gold rules, white title —
the visual signature of the parent project.

verification matrix (11 campaigns, the audit IVP-11) and the claims

the T13 engine bound in the engine part, the updated data dictionary
(32 tables), the figure catalogue (40 + 100 hires) and the multilang
matrix (14/14 ×3).  Both volumes state the hypothesis status with the
machine-enforced status separation.
